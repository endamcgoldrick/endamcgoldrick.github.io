How I work

Joiners, movers and leavers are a big share of any support queue. This is the checklist I follow for each.

Someone starts

Ready to work on day one.

  1. Entra IDCreate the account and add the right groups, so access follows the role.
  2. LicensingAssign the Microsoft 365 licence the job needs.
  3. AutopilotRegister the laptop so it sets itself up at first sign-in.
  4. Intune and DefenderCompliance, configuration and security policies apply before any files are opened.
  5. Teams and SharePointThe team's channels and sites are there on day one.

Someone changes team

Access moves with them.

  1. Entra IDUpdate department, manager and group membership.
  2. Teams and SharePointAdd the new team's sites and channels, remove the old ones.
  3. LicensingCheck the licence still fits the work.
  4. IntuneConfirm the device picks up the policies for the new role or office.

Someone leaves

Access closed the same day, nothing lost.

  1. Entra IDBlock sign-in and revoke active sessions.
  2. Exchange OnlineConvert or hand over the mailbox, following company policy.
  3. OneDrivePass files to the manager before anything is removed.
  4. LicensingRelease the licences so the company stops paying for them.
  5. IntuneWipe the device and prepare it for the next person.
